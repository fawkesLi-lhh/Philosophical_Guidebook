The philosophy defined in this article is as follows: Firstly, all knowledge is philosophy. Among them, all that is verified solely through formal verification is mathematics, and that which is both verified by mathematical means and verified through reality is science. I will trace the evolution history of philosophy based on this framework.

The purpose of this project is to, based on the aforementioned definition, sort out the works of various philosophers and extract their viewpoints and concept definitions from their writings, forming a DAG network of viewpoints and concepts. This enables users to input any viewpoint or concept and, through the project, find the upstream sources, downstream developments, and counterarguments of the viewpoint or concept. The entire knowledge graph can be displayed through the front end, and different schools of thought's research can be folded and scaled. The graph can be viewed from different dimensions, providing users with multiple perspectives to view the evolution of the entire philosophical history. This facilitates finding the source of any unfamiliar viewpoint, as well as its possible derivatives and counterarguments, even when inputting any unfamiliar viewpoint.
